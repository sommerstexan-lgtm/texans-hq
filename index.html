About Texans HQ

What this is
A personal Houston Texans app built for a father and son to follow the team together. It is not an official NFL or Texans product.

What it gives you

  • Game Center — scoreboard, situation, tendencies, injuries, opponent notes, and the Next Play Lean.
  • Next Play Lean — a situation-based guess (run vs pass and more detail) so you can argue about what Houston should call. It uses 2025 Texans tendencies plus down, distance, field position, score, and clock.
  • Play-by-Play — drive and play list (demo now; real feed later).
  • Schedule — full 2026 preseason, regular season, and later postseason.
  • Roster — searchable by name, number, or position with short notes.
  • Stats, Camp, News, Videos — team snapshot, training-camp updates, headlines, and ranked video picks.
  • Notes — private notes that stay only on this device.

Next Play Lean — important rules

  • It is a lean, not a guarantee. Use it for discussion, not as fact.
  • Accuracy is tracked automatically when the next play is known.
  • Preseason never counts toward the accuracy numbers or any learning.
  • Only regular-season and postseason results are kept for analysis.

Current limitations

  • Right now the “live” game is a built-in demo. Real live play-by-play for actual 2026 games is not connected yet.
  • Roster and depth notes are based on public training-camp information and will change after cuts.
  • News and videos depend on public feeds and may be delayed or incomplete when offline.
  • Win probability and some stats are simplified for quick reading, not full models.

Privacy
Password-protected. Notes and accuracy logs stay on this device only. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
